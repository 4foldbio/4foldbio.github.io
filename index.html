Our mission

Every year, millions of cancer patients receive a late or incorrect diagnosis, leading to invasive, unnecessary treatments that devastate lives and drain healthcare resources.

At 4fold, we are changing that by developing a low-cost, highly accurate diagnostics platform that analyses protein and DNA markers in a single test. We will catch cancers that other tests miss and dramatically reduce false alarms.

Our fully automated, sample-to-answer device will require minimal training, making it simple enough for staff at a GP surgery or pharmacy to use, yet engineered to scale for massive volumes in centralised labs.

We are eliminating the diagnostic errors that cost lives. Our mission is to deliver more accurate answers in an affordable way and save more than 100,000 lives every year.

How we’re doing it

Current cancer screening tools force an unacceptable compromise: they are either too expensive for widespread use, or too limited in the biomarkers they measure, resulting in missed diagnoses and false alarms. 4fold is breaking that paradigm. Through novel science and aggressive engineering, we are eliminating the trade-off between accessibility and accuracy.

  • Ultra-multiplexed chemistry

    Our novel detection chemistry allows for the simultaneous detection and quantification of hundreds of nucleic acid or protein targets in parallel, delivering vastly more data than traditional molecular diagnostics.

  • Cost-effective hardware

    By leveraging consumer-electronics supply chains and a modular design, we are building a rugged, highly-accessible instrument that dramatically lowers the barrier to entry for clinics and labs.

  • Frictionless workflow

    A true sample-to-answer system. Using an ultra-simple consumable, our platform will be fully automated from raw samples, requiring minimal training, and delivering actionable results in under 30 minutes.

The “node” architecture

The 4fold system is designed to scale effortlessly based on the user’s needs. Every instrument is a self-contained node that can operate entirely on its own or cluster together for high throughput.

  • Point of need

    A single node is compact and accessible enough to operate at the point of need in a local GP’s office, pharmacy, or clinic. This allows for rapid, on-site testing with immediate results.

  • Centralised testing

    Multiple nodes can be easily stacked to create a high-throughput powerhouse for centralised clinical testing laboratories. Units attach in seconds, automatically sharing power and data with no complex wiring or configuration.
